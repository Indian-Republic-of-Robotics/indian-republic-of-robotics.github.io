Indian Republic of Robotics is a people-powered community for students, researchers and working professionals across India who work in robotics (and industrial automation).

Join on Discord Join on Reddit

What you can do here

  • Share your work

    Students building robots can show their projects and get feedback. Researchers and working professionals can share what they are working on too.

  • Discuss everything robotics

    Ask questions, trade ideas and learn from people across hardware, software, controls, firmware and automation.

  • Find events and jobs

    Meetups, workshops, hackathons, competitions and job openings related to robotics are shared in the community, in Bangalore and across India.

Who it's for

  • Students

    Anyone who wants to learn robotics or is passionate about it. Share what you are building and learn from people who have built similar things.

  • Researchers

    Academics and research scholars working on robotics, automation and related fields. Share your findings and connect with people who can put them to use.

  • Working professionals

    Any engineer working in a robotics company. Robotics is a multidisciplinary field, so you might be any of these:

    • Controls and instrumentation engineer
    • Firmware engineer
    • Software engineer
    • Mechanical engineer
    • Electronics engineer

    Whatever your background, if you work on robots, you are a robotics engineer, and you belong here.

Powered by people like you

Everything here is run by community members who give their time because they love robotics. If you would like to help with any of these, we would love to hear from you:

  • Organizing or hosting a meetup in your city
  • Sharing robotics events happening near you
  • Moderating the Discord and Reddit communities
  • Design, social media and outreach

Ground rules

  • Be kind and patient. Everyone here started as a beginner.
  • Share your work and give credit to others.
  • No spam or unsolicited promotion.
  • Safety first: when discussing hardware, mains power or industrial equipment, always mention the risks.
