# Indian Republic of Robotics

A people-powered community for students, researchers and working professionals across India who work in robotics (and industrial automation).

## What you can do

- **Share your work.** Show your projects and get feedback.
- **Discuss everything robotics.** Ask questions, trade ideas and learn from each other.
- **Find events and jobs.** Meetups, workshops, hackathons, competitions and job openings in Bangalore and across India.

## About this repository

This repository holds the community website. The page is `index.html`, the logo is `logo.png`, and the site is published with GitHub Pages.

## Gallery

Photos in the `gallery` folder appear on the website automatically. Name files like `2026-12-12-first-meetup-group-photo.jpg`, and the website turns the name into a caption.

## Get involved

Everything here is run by community members who give their time because they love robotics. If you would like to help, email us or say hello on Discord.
