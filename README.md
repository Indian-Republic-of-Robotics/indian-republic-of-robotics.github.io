# Indian Republic of Robotics

A people-powered community for students, researchers and working professionals across India who work in robotics (and industrial automation).

## What you can do

- **Share your work.** Show your projects and get feedback.
- **Discuss everything robotics.** Ask questions, trade ideas and learn from each other.
- **Find events and jobs.** Meetups, workshops, hackathons, competitions and job openings in Bangalore and across India.
